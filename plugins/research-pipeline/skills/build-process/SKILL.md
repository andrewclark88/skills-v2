# Build Process

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/docs/build-process.md` completely and apply it to the
current task.

The essential boundaries are:

- Agile Workflow owns `.work/` and delivery.
- Research Pipeline owns project process and knowledge discovery.
- Agentic Research owns grounded research and `.research/` conventions.
- Workbench is optional prior art, not the active workflow.
- Consequential human decisions remain explicit; routine reversible choices
  belong to the agent inside settled scope.
- Ceremony and review depth follow risk and decision consequence.

When another skill invokes this reference, continue that skill after loading
the methodology; do not create a second workflow or duplicate its artifacts.
